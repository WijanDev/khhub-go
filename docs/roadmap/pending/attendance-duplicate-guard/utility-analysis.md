# Utility analysis

- **Score:** 4
- **Scale:** 1 = low … 5 = high

## Justification

Weekly averages go to the branch. A duplicate midweek, a fake weekend on an assembly date, or a Memorial stuffed into `weekend` all corrupt that copy. Special visits and assemblies are few per year but they are exactly when the secretary would otherwise invent or skip a row. A global unique would also block two congregations on the same Saturday. Skipping the calendar leaves counts untrustworthy after the first schedule change, Memorial, or assembly weekend.
